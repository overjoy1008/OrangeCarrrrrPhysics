Mew — the guest karts' engine set
=================================

Not a recovered preset. The eight engine sets beside this one came out of
sound_fx_engine; this one did not, and it is here so the three guest cats stop
borrowing the 9th generation's motor.

Source
------
A 65 s vertical video, "Engine is running!" (720x1280, h264/aac), of a cat
purring on its owner's lap. Brought in by hand; the file carries no author, no
licence and no link, so THIS IS NOT CLEARED FOR DISTRIBUTION. Find the original
and record it here before this repository is published, or replace the samples.

The same warning the banana model carries applies with more force: that one at
least names its author.

What was done to it
-------------------
Everything below is measurement, not taste, except where it says otherwise.

  region     58.0-64.0 s, the loudest stretch with the steadiest pulse
             (purr rate 27.0-27.8 Hz, autocorrelation 0.39-0.53)
  high-pass  30 Hz, to drop the DC and the room
  loop       0.734 s from 59.98 s, chosen by maximising the correlation between
             a 50 ms head window and the same window one loop length later
             (0.807) — 20 purr cycles, against the recovered classic loop's 0.782 s
  seam       20 ms equal-power crossfade
  rate       44100 -> 22050, low-passed at 10.2 kHz first, then decimated by 2
  level      scaled to classic's rms of 0.117

The cat did not need a pitch correction: its pulse is 27.4 Hz against the
recovered classic loop's 27.7, so the speed ramp lands it on the same firing
rate it lands the demo's engine on, at every point of the ramp.

Two things were then done on top of that, and both are taste rather than
measurement — asked for after listening to the sweep above:

  x1.30 rate  the loop resampled to 0.5646 s, so the pulse reads 35.6 Hz at
              playback rate 1.0 instead of 27.4. A resample rather than a change
              to MotorSlope or MotorBase: those two are recovered constants and
              the tachometer reads the same number the mixer does, so moving them
              would move the dial and every other kart with it. The sample
              carries the shift; the law does not know it happened.
  x1.50 level rms 0.1752, peak 0.766. Headroom is the reason this is done to the
              sample too — the volume law tops out at 1.0 and cannot be asked
              for more.

The resample step is L/N exactly, N being the rounded new length, so the loop
still wraps onto itself and the crossfaded seam survives it.

boosterPlay.wav
---------------
Also the purr, and the only other slot that is. It is the loop held while the
player revs on the start line, so it is the motor rather than a boost — a cat
that idles as a cat should rev as one. The same loop played at 1.30, held for
1.241 s to match the length classic's is, with a 30 ms crossfade at the seam and
scaled to rms 0.20.

The rate is taste, unlike everything above it. It was picked to sit above the
idle the speed ramp gives a standing kart (0.25) without reaching the note the
kart makes once it is moving, so the rev reads as a rev.

All three cats get it: nothing overrides this slot the way BoosterOverride
overrides the booster.

booster.wav, boosterDrift.wav
-----------------------------
Byte copies of jiu's, which is what the guests were boosting with before this
folder existed. Those two are one-shots fired by an event rather than the motor,
they were never the thing that did not fit, and inventing them out of the same
purr would have been taste standing where a measurement belongs. A bike folder
carries a copy of its car's last two slots for the same reason.

Only Maxwell hears them anyway — oiia and banana bring their own booster sample.
All three keep the shared drift, crash and shock.
